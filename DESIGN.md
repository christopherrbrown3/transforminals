# Visual direction

The user's pinned direction is a colorful, blocky, toy-like adventure. The 3D world fills the screen from the first viewport. Warm cream field-guide controls and forest-green labels sit around the edges, leaving the center for play. Chunky silhouettes, pastel skies, saturated ground, and rounded bevels make the world feel like a handmade toy set. The title screen looks into the actual playable Meadow, never a disconnected marketing image.

Typography: self-hosted Fredoka for the title and rounded controls; highly readable system sans for longer dialogue. Icons use a consistent authored SVG stroke. Touch controls are big physical-looking buttons. A single compact notification slot handles arrival, rescue, and deliberate NPC interactions. Only the closest nearby cage or portal receives a world label. Reduced motion removes camera flourish and reduces particles.

## Surface: game

Mode: Experience. Entry: start or continue straight into Meadow. Path: begin exploring immediately, jump onto a broad cage, rescue a fox, choose its portrait, try its dash. The October 18 polish request removes the automatic introduction; an optional How to play panel lives in Pause. Main controls: forms on left; movement and star on right. Map and settings stay at the top. No tiny actions or precision jumps.

## Art contract

Creature models face negative Z, have feet at Y=0 and use Three.js standard materials. Ground is Y=0. Human height 1.65 units. Rescue cages are 3.6 units wide and 1.2 high. Decorative obstacles leave clear routes. Region radius is 42 units, with roads/portals at the rim and recognizable center landmarks.
