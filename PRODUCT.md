# Transforminals

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User specified TypeScript, Three.js, Vite, local saves, Vitest, Playwright, GitHub Pages and an offline PWA.

## Users

Calvin, age seven turning eight, and children ages 6–10 playing primarily on an iPad in landscape.

## Product Purpose

Rescue animals by jumping onto cages, become those animals, explore ten connected environments, and win easy, silly boss encounters without ever dying.

## Operating Context

Touch-first third-person adventure; desktop keyboard support. All runtime code, assets and audio are local. No accounts, cloud saves or external APIs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Required animal rescues gate a five-head-stomp final fight with three visible hearts. All biomes remain reachable in human form. Saves survive updates. Active gameplay must never reload for an update. GitHub identity is christopherrbrown3.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly specified a colorful, blocky, toy-like world, expressive friendly animals, original characters, magical transformations, playful chaos and easy controls. Calvin is the designer. No mermaids; boy sirens sing original jazz.

## Evidence on Hand

The complete user brief is preserved in docs/ORIGINAL_BRIEF.md. Physical iPad validation is not yet available.

## Product Principles

- Keep exploration forgiving, lively and full of short journeys.
- Finish and validate one playable slice before expanding.
- Give every rescue, transformation and boss hit clear feedback.
- Use original art and music; preserve progress locally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large touch targets, readable text, no death or punishment, optional audio and reduced motion. No timed reading.
