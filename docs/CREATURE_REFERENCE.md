# Creature reference notes

Transforminals' creatures are original, low-poly toy models built from local Three.js geometry in `src/game/animalModels.ts` and `src/game/creatures.ts`. Written references below informed a few recognizable silhouette cues; they were not used as model, texture, or animation assets. The references are general anatomy and identification material, not a claim that the models are scientifically accurate. Proportions, colors, materials, expressions, scale, motion, powers, and fantasy traits are deliberately simplified or invented for a playful game.

## Geometry checks

`src/game/creatures.test.ts` validates all 22 rescue models for finite, non-degenerate geometry and bounded idle/moving poses. It also fits each model to the game cage dimensions and checks multiple idle phases against the cage floor, roof, and sides. These are geometry regressions, not a validation of biological fidelity or visual quality on every device.

Additional fish identification checks: [Florida Museum's great barracuda profile](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/species-profiles/great-barracuda/) describes its slender body, widely separated dorsal fins and projecting lower jaw; these informed the narrow toy sculpture. [Smithsonian's red-bellied piranha profile](https://www.nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/red-bellied-piranha) provides the contrasting compact fish and warm underside reference. Both are written anatomy references only; no page imagery is packaged.
